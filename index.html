There is no installer. Download, extract and start.

Frequently asked questions.

Is it really free? (Kya yeh software free hai?)

Yes. It is free to download and use, with no subscription, no monthly fee and no hidden charges.

Does it need internet? (Internet chahiye?)

No. PharmacyERP works 100% offline with a local SQLite database on your computer, so your data stays on your machine.

Which thermal printer works?

Any 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer that installs as a Windows printer, including POS-58, Xprinter XP-58 and EPSON TM-T20. Enter its exact name from Windows Printers & Scanners in Settings → Local Config.

Can I change the receipt header?

Yes. Go to Settings → Setup Form → Print Header and enter your pharmacy name, address, phone and any extra text. It appears on every receipt.

How do I change my password?

Log in, open Settings → Change Password, enter your current password and then the new one twice. Managers can change their own passwords the same way.

Is Khata / Udhaar supported?

Yes. Register regular customers in Accounts. At checkout, select the customer and press F11. Take part payment in cash and the balance goes to their Khata, tracked in the ledger.

How does it handle prescription (Rx) medicines?

Mark a medicine as Prescription Required. The POS highlights it and asks the cashier to confirm Rx verification and enter a prescription reference before checkout, which helps you keep DRAP-ready records.

What if the PC shuts down during a sale?

The cart is saved automatically every 10 seconds. After restarting, click Recover Cart on the POS to get your items back exactly as they were.

Can I install it on a phone or Mac?

PharmacyERP is a Windows desktop app for Windows 10 and 11. Open this page on your pharmacy PC to download it.
